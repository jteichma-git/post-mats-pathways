A free, self-paced introductory course on AGI safety from Google DeepMind's safety research team, consisting of ~75 minutes of short recorded talks and exercises with an accompanying slide deck and exercise workbook. Outsider's Roadmap to AI Safety (LessWrong)

A comprehensive guide written in July 2025 for professionals transitioning into AI safety research from technical fields. Covers three main domains: interpretability research (reverse-engineering AI systems to understand how they work), alignment research (ensuring AI systems remain beneficial and share human values), and AI control & governance (technical policy for advanced systems). Synthesizes insights from Neel Nanda's mechanistic interpretability resources, 80,000 Hours career research, and the broader alignment community, with practical advice on roles (research engineers vs. scientists converging into 'full-stack researchers'), required skills, and how newcomers can contribute to the field.

Anthropic Fellows Program

The Anthropic Fellows Program provides funding and mentorship for engineers and researchers to investigate high-priority AI safety research questions including scalable oversight, adversarial robustness, AI control, model organisms, mechanistic interpretability, AI security, and model welfare. Fellows receive a weekly stipend of $3,850 USD (£2,310 GBP / $4,300 CAD), ~$15K/month in compute funding, and close mentorship from Anthropic researchers over a 4-month period. Over 80% of fellows in the first cohort produced papers, and over 40% subsequently joined Anthropic full-time; no PhD, prior ML experience, or published papers required.
