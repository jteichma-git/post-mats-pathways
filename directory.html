1. Career Resources

Technical safety & governance roles at labs and orgs. The AI safety roles significantly outpaced general tech hiring in 2024–25. Internships are a strong entry point.

80,000 Hours Job Board

A handpicked, continuously updated board of high-impact roles, with heavy emphasis on AI safety, AI policy and existential risk. Filter by annualised USD salary, location and role type, exclude career-development roles, save or hide listings, and get matches based on your background. Includes daily/weekly job alerts, free 1-1 career advice and an AI advisor chat.
Updated continuously

80K Hours AI Safety Career Review

A 80,000 Hours career review on technical AI safety research, covering empirical (open to strong software engineers, even without ML backgrounds) and theoretical paths (for strong maths/theoretical CS backgrounds or top-10 ML PhD candidates). Covers impact, downsides, earnings, personal fit, PhD decisions, job hunting and key organisations. Notes only ~300 people worked on the field as of 2022.

A continuously updated AI safety job board listing 575 open roles (plus 5 more) across research, policy, engineering, operations, communications and security. Many roles are remote and non-technical.
Updated continuously

Outputs include an open-source multi-agent harness, the xorformer theorem ledger and an oral paper at the AutoMLR Workshop at NeurIPS 2026. No formal application — join the Discord or email hello@verifiedmechanisms.ai.

ALIGN, a Japanese non-profit focused on AI alignment research, community building and outreach, is recruiting volunteer staff for event operations, community management, PR/social media, research and writing, website upkeep and organizational administration. Google DeepMind: AGI Safety Short Course

A free, self-paced introductory course on AGI safety from Google DeepMind's safety research team, comprising ~75 minutes of short recorded talks and exercises plus a slide deck and exercise workbook. It covers the path to superhuman capabilities, risks from deliberate planning and instrumental subgoals, how misaligned goals arise (specification gaming vs. goal misgeneralization), DeepMind's technical alignment approach (amplified oversight, robust training & monitoring, security & control, interpretability, safer design patterns, alignment stress tests), and its governance approach including frontier safety practices and dangerous capability evaluations. Aimed at students, researchers and professionals who want an accessible one-hour introduction to AI alignment.
Self-paced — no deadline

MIT AI Risk Initiative

The MIT AI Risk Initiative (MIT FutureTech) publishes open, CC BY 4.0 data and frameworks for identifying, prioritizing and managing AI risks: a living repository of 1,700+ risks from 65 frameworks, an AI Incident Tracker, a global governance database, 2,206 mitigations from 47 frameworks, disclosures from the 200 largest companies, a survey of 272 experts, existential-risk scenarios, and the AI Risk Navigator. No applications — newsletter, blog, and partnership contact.

ML4Good Technical Bootcamp

ML4Good's flagship technical AI safety bootcamp: a free, residential programme covering transformers from scratch, LLM agents, interpretability, RLHF, evaluations, technical governance and a 2-day capstone with mentorship. Food, accommodation and teaching are free; travel may be reimbursed. Coding is ~25-30% of the curriculum; cohorts are regional and run in English.
Not yet announced — join notification list

Outsider's Roadmap to AI Safety (LessWrong)

A comprehensive guide published in July 2025 for professionals transitioning into AI safety research from technical backgrounds such as software engineering, physics, or data science. Covers three critical domains: interpretability research (reverse-engineering AI systems to understand their inner workings), alignment research (ensuring AI systems remain beneficial and share human values), and AI control & governance (technical policy for advanced systems). Written by someone actively making the transition, synthesizing insights from Neel Nanda's mechanistic interpretability resources, 80,000 Hours career research, and the broader alignment community, with practical advice on evolving roles (the shift from separate research engineers/scientists toward 'full-stack researchers'), required skills, and pathways for newcomers to contribute meaningfully to the field.

Successif

Successif offers free, personalized career advising for experienced professionals moving into AI risk reduction. Support includes a dedicated advisor, resume and application feedback, mock interviews, expert-led workshops and self-paced courses, direct introductions to AI risk organizations with early job access, and a peer community via Discord and events. Specialized tracks cover AI policy strategy and women/underrepresented groups.
Rolling

3. Fellowships & Research Programs

AI safety fellowships with stipends, mentorship, and compute. Many programs run multiple cohorts per year across the US and UK.

AI Safety Camp

AI Safety Camp's 12th edition is an online, part-time research program where participants join one of 32 preselected projects spanning stop/pause AI, policy and governance, agentic risk and oversight, evaluations, mech-interp, and AI safety education. Team members commit 10 hours/week plus weekly meetings, running January–April 2027 and ending with an online final-presentations conference. Apply to specific projects or submit one generic application.
November 29, 2026 (team member applications)

Iliad Intensive / Fellowship

Iliad runs in-house AI alignment research and training programs. The Iliad Fellowship helps technically strong scholars pivot into mathematical and theoretical alignment over ~12 weeks in London or the Bay Area; Fellows must first complete the month-long Iliad Intensive. Research spans learning theory, debate, heuristic arguments, data attribution, agency, and neural network geometry. About half of Fellows receive a six-month extension.
Dec 16, 2026 (February 2027 Fellowship)

Astera Residency

A 12–18 month, fully funded residency in Emeryville, California for entrepreneurial builders creating public goods across AI research, neuroscience, life sciences, open science, and deep tech. Residents receive $125K–$250K salary plus up to $2M project budget, compute via Voltage Park, and dedicated workspace.
January 7, 2027 (Spring 2027 cohort)

SPAR

SPAR, run by Kairos, is a 3-month remote fellowship pairing students and professionals with experienced mentors on AI safety, policy, biosecurity, communications and fieldbuilding projects. Fellows commit 5–40 hours/week alongside other work and present at a virtual Demo Day with $7,000 in prizes. Open to all experience levels; an expression-of-interest form is live for Spring 2027.
Spring 2027 applications open December 16, 2026 and close January 13, 2027 (mentor/org applications close November 30, 2026)

AE Studio — AI Alignment Research Grants

The AI Alignment Foundation (spun out of AE Studio) funds high-risk, high-reward alignment research beyond interpretability, evals, and control, pairing researchers with dedicated engineering teams that can execute ideas with as little as one hour per week of your time. Open to alignment researchers and technical experts from other fields; applications accepted via open form.

Anthropic Fellows funds and mentors engineers and researchers working on priority AI safety questions — scalable oversight, AI control, model organisms, interpretability, AI security, and model welfare. 4. Grants & Funding

Fund your own research through grants, regranters, and open RFPs. Many programs offer rolling applications with fast turnaround.

Coefficient Giving — Project Tailwind

Project Tailwind is Coefficient Giving's call for founders to launch ambitious new AI safety organisations, funded across three tiers: pre-seed $200K–$2M, seed $2M–$20M, and scale $20M–$200M, typically covering 18–36 months of runway. Founders can pitch their own idea or tackle one of Coefficient's published unsolved problems in AI safety.
Rolling

EA Funds — Transformative AI Fund

EA Funds' Transformative AI Fund makes early-stage, fast grants to individuals, new organizations, and existing orgs launching new projects on risks from advanced AI, plus field-building, training, policy, forecasting and advocacy work. Grants are typically $10k–$150k and rarely exceed $300k. Applications are always open; biosecurity and nuclear work are out of scope.
Rolling — always open

Falcon Fund (Manifund)

Manifund-hosted animal welfare fund led by regrantor Marcus Abramovitch, making rapid (under 1 week) early-stage grants of $25K–$150K, focused on the intersection of animal welfare and transformative AI. Priorities include animal harm benchmarks, animal welfare constitutions, AI-in-factory-farming watchdog work, and raising animal salience in AI labs. Grantmaking is mostly outbound; contact marcus.s.abramovitch@gmail.com.
Rolling

Manifund Regranting

Manifund pairs AI safety experts with independent regranting budgets of $100K–$350K, sourced from their own networks or Manifund's open call. Single-owner budgets mean grants land in days, not months, with example regrants from $11K to $200K. Its 501c3 fiscal sponsorship covers individuals, unregistered projects and (pending diligence) for-profits; all grants and rationales are published publicly.
Rolling

Paradigm 3 Research Fund

Paradigm 3's research fund backs external work measuring and forecasting AI capabilities and impacts. Current grantees estimate AI externalities, decompose benchmark gains into cheating/memorization/generalization, and study when slowing AI progress is desirable. They welcome proposals on centaur evals, 'guardian angel' user-aligned models, virtue-vs-rules alignment, scaling and 'unhobbling', automated science, compute growth, job displacement, and recursive self-improvement — email hi@paradigm3.org.
Rolling

Thinking Machines Lab — Safety Research Grants

Thinking Machines Lab offers Tinker grants of up to $50,000 in API/compute credits for safety research on open-weight models, launched alongside its Inkling and Inkling-Small releases. Priority areas include differential defense-vs-offense capability uplift, hazardous-data classifiers, tamper-resistant safety training, emergent misalignment, reward hacking, and worst-case risk forecasting. The list is explicitly non-exhaustive.
Rolling

5. Incubators & Startup Programs

Launch a new org or project via incubators and grants. Multiple programs offer stipends, mentorship, co-founder matching, and seed funding.

BlueDot Impact — Incubator Week

BlueDot Impact's Incubator Week is a 5-day, all-expenses-paid founder sprint in San Francisco where you and a potential co-founder test-drive building an AI safety organization — covering founding 101, threat modelling, interventions, and a final pitch worth up to $200k in seed funding. For people in AI safety, biosecurity, cyber or other catastrophic-risk fields; no polished idea needed.
Not yet announced — express interest anytime

Catalyze Impact

Catalyze Impact is a 501(c)(3) non-profit incubator that helps exceptional founders launch AI safety and resilience organizations, helping them pick the right problem and connecting them to funders and domain experts. Since 2023 it has helped launch 20+ organizations that raised over $190M. Its two Funding Networks (investors and grantmakers, $50K+/yr) run 2–4 rounds a year.
Rolling — get in touch

Halcyon Futures

Halcyon Futures is a grant-maker, incubator and investment firm backing exceptional founders building in AI safety, cybersecurity and biosecurity. It combines grants, incubation support and venture investment to build resilience for the era of transformative AI. Prospective founders can reach the team directly at hello@halcyonfutures.org.
Rolling

ImpactOps

Impact Ops is an operations consultancy giving hands-on specialist support to high-impact organizations across AI safety, global health, animal welfare and climate. Services cover six areas: finance, recruitment, entity setup and fiscal-sponsor spin-outs, donor/grantee due diligence, ops audits, and systems implementation. Clients include CLTR, BlueDot Impact, Foresight Institute, Longview Philanthropy and The Earthshot Prize. Careers and contact pages available.

Rethink Priorities Special Projects

Rethink Priorities' Special Projects Program offers fiscal sponsorship and end-to-end operations for high-impact initiatives: tax-exempt grant receipt, accounting, US/UK or Employer-of-Record hiring, visa support, fundraising coordination, and event planning. Fees typically around 14% of project costs. Through end of 2025: 195 expressions of interest, 15 projects supported, $19.7M expended, 114 sponsee staff onboarded.
Rolling

Seldon Lab

Seldon Lab is an accelerator and venture studio backing founders building AI security and existential safety technologies for world-scale AGI deployment. Beyond capital and mentorship, the team publishes research, builds systems, and deploys cyber-physical infrastructure. Portfolio companies include Lucid Computing and Andon Labs; applications are accepted on a rolling basis via the Apply Now page.
Rolling
