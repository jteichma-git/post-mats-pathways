Outsider's Roadmap to AI Safety (LessWrong)
A comprehensive guide written in July 2025 for professionals transitioning into AI safety research from other technical fields. Covers three main domains: interpretability research (reverse-engineering AI systems), alignment research (ensuring AI systems remain beneficial and share human values), and AI control & governance (technical policy for advanced systems). The guide synthesizes insights from Neel Nanda's mechanistic interpretability resources, 80,000 Hours career research, and the broader alignment community, with practical advice on roles (research engineers vs. scientists, converging into "full-stack researchers"), skills needed, and how newcomers can contribute to the field.
