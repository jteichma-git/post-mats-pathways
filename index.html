Applications are open for three new important AI safety positions in New York’s government.

Housed within NY’s Department of Financial Services, the newly created Office of Digital Innovation, Governance, Integrity & Trust (DIGIT) will operate as NY’s AI regulator and implement the recently enacted Responsible Artificial Intelligence Safety & Education (RAISE) Act (one of the most important frontier AI laws enacted in the US). DIGIT is equipped with a $9M budget and is now hiring for three roles critical to implementing the RAISE Act:

Associate Counsel
The Associate Counsel “addresses legal issues related to [the RAISE Act],” provides “analysis and counsel” on proposed regulations and legislation, and “formulates legal and policy recommendations.” Preferred qualifications include “four years of relevant professional legal experience,” and experience in AI governance, cybersecurity, or data privacy.
Salary: $120,471 - $152,238 (commensurate with experience), with an additional $4,150 if you work from the NYC area
Position type: Full-time, permanent

Data Analyst 3
The Data Analyst 3 coordinates “the assessment of data from RAISE Act submissions,” “translates technical details from RAISE Act filings and related information into… reports and briefings for attorneys, policymakers, and operational team members,” “leads technical discussions with frontier model developers,” and “supervises a team of subordinate staff.” Applicants must have a bachelor’s degree plus 5 years of data or modeling experience (or another combination of education and experience). “Preferred qualifications include “working proficiency in SQL and Python or R,” and “advanced knowledge of frontier AI evaluation, security, and safety practices.”
Salary: $111,708 with periodic increases up to $137,590, with an additional $4,150 if you work from the NYC area
Position type: Temporary, but likely to become permanent

Data Analyst 2
The Data Analyst 2 “produces business intelligence solutions by querying data from RAISE Act submissions,” “devises methods for identifying data patterns and trends,” “drafts new functions or applications,” and “creates written reports and effectively communicates/presents results.” Applicants must have a bachelor’s degree plus 3 years of data or modeling experience (or another combination of education and experience). “Preferred qualifications include “foundational skills in SQL… and Excel,” “exposure to Python or R,” and “familiarity with frontier AI concepts.”
Salary: $90,582 with periodic increases up to $114,585, with an additional $4,150 if you work from the NYC area
Position type: Temporary, but likely to become permanent

The roles can be performed from Albany or NYC, and applications are due Monday, 11/30. (The job description for the Associate Counsel role says that it closes on 11/20, but we have been informed that this is a typo.)

Secure AI Project thinks these are important opportunities for folks who want to contribute to frontier AI policy and help ensure that implementation of the RAISE Act goes well.

If you’re interested in applying, we encourage you to email implementation@secureaiproject.org, as we can give more context on the roles. Feel free to forward this to candidates you think may be a good fit!
